Cisco Restoration & AIS Threats

An assessment of the threats aquatic invasive species pose to the restoration of Cisco in Lake Erie, supporting the Coregonine Restoration Framework.

IAGLR Conference Presentations

Research presented on how aquatic invasive species threaten the restoration of Cisco in Lake Erie. Geospatial analysis indicates that out of 2153.5 square miles of potential habitat, 444.97 square miles are notably affected by AIS. Key species impacting Cisco include rainbow smelt, spiny waterflea, sea lamprey, bacterial kidney disease, and alewife.
